My Story

Real estate is about belonging, not just buildings.

Nine years ago, my family and I relocated to Texas. I know first-hand how overwhelming that journey can be: the neighborhoods, the schools, the hundred small decisions that add up to a life. That experience is what I now pour back into every client I work with.

With Magnolia Realty behind me, I pair expert guidance with a deep love for the greater Austin area. I'll help you understand the neighborhoods, the schools, and all the little spots that make a community feel like yours.

When I'm not at the closing table, you'll find me cheering on my three energetic boys at the baseball field, soaking up the sun at the lake, or baking something sweet. To me, a house isn't just a place. It's where life happens.
