Leander

Leander has grown from a quiet bedroom community into one of the most sought-after places in the Austin metro. Families come for strong schools, master-planned communities and a small-town feel that still sits close to the big city.

Schools

Leander ISD earned an 88/100 accountability score from the Texas Education Agency for 2024–25, with a 97% graduation rate across 40+ campuses. School assignments vary by address, so check the LISD attendance zone map.

  • Rouse High SchoolStrong AP and dual credit
  • Glenn High SchoolAthletics, fine arts, STEM
  • Leander High SchoolOne of the district's largest

Getting around

The 183A Toll Road is the everyday route into North Austin. Leander is also the northern end of Capital Metro's MetroRail line, with free parking at Leander Station.

  • The Domain~25 min · 183A Toll
  • Apple campus~30 min · 183A Toll
  • Downtown Austin~40 min drive · ~45 min MetroRail

  • Parkside at Mayfield RanchNewer homes with a Georgetown address and Leander ISD schools.~$500K–$620K

Cedar Park

Cedar Park sits in the Hill Country just northwest of Austin and pairs small-town warmth with big-city access. Buyers love it for established neighborhoods, top-rated schools, great parks and a local scene that keeps getting better.

Schools

Cedar Park is served by two recognized districts, Leander ISD and Round Rock ISD. Most neighborhoods are in Leander ISD, but some, like parts of Avery Ranch, are in Round Rock ISD, so always confirm by address.

  • Cedar Park High SchoolLeander ISD · 9–12
  • Vista Ridge High SchoolLeander ISD · 9–12
  • Henry Middle SchoolLeander ISD · 6–8

Getting around

Cedar Park sits right on 183A and US-183, so North Austin's big employers are an easy drive. Drive times are estimates under normal traffic.

  • The Domain / Apple~20 min · 183A Toll
  • Downtown Austin~35–40 min · 183A + MoPac
  • Austin airport~45 min · 183A + 290/71

Popular neighborhoods

  • Deer Creek RanchSpacious homes on larger lots with a quieter, tucked-away feel.~$550K–$750K+

  • Avery RanchGolf course living close to shopping, dining and major employers.~$500K–$800K+
